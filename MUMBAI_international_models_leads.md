## Why agencies first

- Agency rosters show each model's portfolio and Instagram, so every handle is checked by the agency itself.
- Ask for Mumbai-based international "new faces" (newly signed models). They cost less than established names and need no travel.
- I could not verify individual model handles in this session, so none are listed. Pull them from the rosters the agencies send.
