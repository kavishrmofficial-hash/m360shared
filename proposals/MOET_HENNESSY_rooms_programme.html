The idea

Every key account becomes a room where MOËT & CHANDON, CHANDON INDIA and GLENMORANGIE are tasted, talked about and listed. Mask360 designs, builds and runs each room, and reports back by account.

The room is the ad.

Direct liquor advertising is off the table in India. Tasting, people and places carry the brand, so that is where the spend works hardest.

Your accounts are the stage.

Luxury hotels, fine dining rooms, bars and modern trade outlets already hold your guests. Each room is built for one of them and repeats across the rest.

Every room reports.

Listings added, covers served and sell out during each activation window, tracked per account so each rupee maps back.
